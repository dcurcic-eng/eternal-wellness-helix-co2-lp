HOW IT WORKS

Forty-five minutes, start to finish.

Your provider sets the mode to your skin, not to a protocol. CoolPeel for a light reset, Sultra for collagen without surface downtime, Fusion when you want the full correction in one visit.

01

Numb & map

Topical numbing cream goes on while your provider maps the treatment areas and sets the mode, CoolPeel, Sultra or Fusion, to your skin type and goals.

02

Resurface

The handpiece passes over the area. The 10,600 nm beam creates thousands of microscopic treatment zones; the 1,570 nm beam reaches the deep dermis to drive collagen remodeling without breaking the surface.

03

Cool & protect

Immediate cooling and a soothing balm, then your aftercare plan: occlusive ointment, no actives, hard sun protection until the new surface settles.

WHAT RECOVERY LOOKS LIKE

One week, honestly mapped.

Day 0Warmth and redness like a strong sunburn. Cooling and a soothing balm before you leave.
Days 1–2Redness peaks with mild swelling; skin feels sandpapery. CoolPeel is essentially done here.
Days 3–4Light flaking and dryness. Most patients return to normal activity and work.
Days 5–7A fresh surface, redness fading. Fusion fully settles at the end of this window.
Weeks 1–2First visible smoothing and an evener tone as new collagen starts to lay down.

Months 3–6, peak result as collagen remodeling continues. Results hold 1–3+ years. Side effects are limited to redness, mild swelling, dryness and light peeling; post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is rare (under 5% of properly screened patients).
